Overview

What is vividXperience?

vividXperience is a Vision Pro application that lets users experience the vibrant atmosphere of Vivid Sydney from home. Through immersive AR/VR, users explore digital replicas of light installations, navigate a 3D panoramic map of the festival, and interact with key highlights — making the festival accessible to anyone who can't attend in person.

Key Features

Four entry points

  1. 3D Panoramic Map

    A bird's-eye view of the festival. Spin, zoom, and tap any landmark to drop into its immersive scene.

  2. Event Discovery

    Browse light shows, drone performances, and creative workshops by category. Cards animate forward on hover.

  3. Immersive Experience

    Step inside Vivid Sydney. Tap to summon a sidebar with pause, scene-switch, and a progress bar — without breaking the spell.

  4. Cross-device Album

    Record AR moments on your phone, then replay them in 3D inside Vision Pro. One album, two devices, one memory.
